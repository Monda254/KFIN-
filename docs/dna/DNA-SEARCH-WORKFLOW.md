# KFIN DNA Search & Review Workflow Specification

## 1. Candidate Review Lifecycle
Candidate results generated by the search engine enter a controlled review workflow:

```text
  CANDIDATE ──► UNDER_REVIEW ──┬──► TECHNICALLY_CONFIRMED
                               ├──► EXCLUDED
                               ├──► INCONCLUSIVE
                               └──► REQUIRES_RETEST
```

## 2. Four-Eyes Independent Review (Separation of Duties)
- Significant match decisions require independent peer review by an accredited senior lab analyst or director.
- Analysts cannot approve or confirm match candidates generated from profiles they authored without secondary confirmation where SoD policy applies.

## 3. Candidate Review Outcomes
- **`TECHNICALLY_CONFIRMED`**: Forensic scientist confirms loci alignment and LR score; candidate becomes an authorized forensic intelligence lead.
- **`EXCLUDED`**: Locus mismatch or biological exclusion confirmed.
- **`INCONCLUSIVE`**: Insufficient loci resolution or degraded profile prevents definitive technical conclusion.
- **`REQUIRES_RETEST`**: Re-amplification or re-extraction required from primary biological sample.
